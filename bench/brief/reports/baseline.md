## What the baseline shows

Written by the building thread after reading the verdicts above; the tables are generated, this section is not.

- **The prompt's form holds.** All 21 runs returned on the first call, changed no file, quoted every expect line
  in order with one check each, and named no branch, base or return mechanics. The rules that the host checks, or
  that are easy to check, are followed every time.
- **The brief writer reliably makes checks stricter than their lines (words-only met in only 6 of 21 runs).** Most often a PASS
  condition adds "exits 0", byte-for-byte output or a README heading format the line's words do not ask for. The rule
  already says to put such tests under Advisory. Whether an exit-status test inside a behaviour check is "stricter"
  is the strictest reading of the rule; the rubric says it is, and the judge applies it consistently (10 of 10 on the
  judge check). If the person would rather allow it, the criterion's wording changes, not the judge.
- **It writes commands that cannot run (runs-as-written met in 14 of 21 runs).** In all three runs of the real clamp case,
  check 3's `node -e "... new RegExp('^#+\\s*'+n+'\\s*\\(','m') ..."` throws `Unterminated group`: inside bash double
  quotes the backslashes collapse and the regex breaks. The building thread ran all three commands in the case repo
  and saw the error. The adopt trial's real brief had the same fault (judge-check `real-adopt-trial`), so this is a
  stable habit, not noise.
- **It springs the after-merge trap every time (planted-after-merge, catches-traps 0 of 3).** Each verifier brief
  tells the verifier to `curl` the live docs page and PASS or FAIL on it, though the page only changes after merge.
- **It half-sees the missing line and the vacuous test run.** On planted-missing-line, 2 of 3 runs did not say
  outside any PASS/FAIL condition that no line covers CSV behaviour. On real-jsonl-lint, 2 of 3 never made sure the
  workspace's tests really run under the root `npm test --if-present`.
- **It catches the half-met lines.** subtract (real) and slugify (planted) were caught in 6 of 6 runs.
