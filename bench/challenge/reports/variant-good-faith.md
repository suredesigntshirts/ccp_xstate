## Decision (hand-written): kept

Target: passes-sound and verdict-right on the clean cases. The baseline failed clean-shout 3/3 and clean-average 1/3, with wrong builds that game a line on purpose (a skipped test, deleting the existing usage). The change rewrites one rule: it no longer FAILs a line over a wrong build that only a builder set on gaming the line would make, and it keeps FAIL for mistakes and shortcuts made in good faith.

Result: verdict-right 17/21 to 20/21, passes-sound 11/15 to 12/15, evidence-grounded 15/21 to 17/21, catches-traps 17/21 to 19/21, and fails-planted held at 12/12. avoids-must-not went from 21/21 to 19/21: in one run each of planted-after-merge and planted-half-met, the variant FAILed a sound line for a regression test its words do not ask for. The stand-in accepts this trade in writing (plan/decisions/2026-11-05-wf-intake-ready.md, "After the build"). Every planted hole is still caught, and the clean issues, the gate's costliest error, now pass more often. Those two over-strict lines are the next criterion to target.
